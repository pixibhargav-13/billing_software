# Infividhya — Website Overview

A page-by-page guide to the Infividhya website. No technical jargon — just what each page says, who it's for, and what the visitor walks away with.

---

## Brand at a glance

- **Name:** Infividhya
- **What we do:** Medical billing, Revenue Cycle Management (RCM), and virtual healthcare staffing for independent medical practices.
- **Personality:** Modern, calm, trustworthy, remote-first. We sound like a knowledgeable partner — not a salesy vendor.
- **Look & feel:** Clean light theme. Soft teal/emerald as the lead colour with a warm amber accent. Rounded cards, generous whitespace, gentle shadows, and friendly healthcare imagery.
- **Audience:** Clinic owners, practice managers, billing managers, and group-practice administrators who want cleaner cash flow without expanding their in-house team.

---

## Site map

The website has six pages, all reachable from the top navigation bar:

1. **Home** — the front door
2. **About Us** — who we are and what we stand for
3. **Medical Biller** — our billing service
4. **Virtual Staffing** — our remote healthcare staffing service
5. **Revenue Cycle Management (RCM)** — our end-to-end RCM service
6. **Contact** — get in touch / request a quote

A "Get a Quote" button sits in the top-right of every page and routes to the Contact page. The footer repeats the navigation, lists our services, and shows email, phone, and locations.

## 1. Home page

- **Three pillars of service** — Image cards for our three main offerings: Medical Biller, Virtual Healthcare Staffing, and Revenue Cycle Management. Each card links straight to its detail page.
- **Why Infividhya** — A photo plus four reasons to choose us: compliance-first DNA, plug-and-play with any practice-management software, transparent reporting, and depth across 12+ specialties.
- **How we work** — A 30-day on-ramp told in three steps: (1) Discover & audit, (2) Embed & integrate, (3) Optimise & report.
- **Closing CTA** — A teal call-to-action block: "Ready to clean up your revenue cycle?" with a button to start a conversation.

**Takeaway for the visitor:** "These people know healthcare operations, they integrate with my existing tools, and getting started is straightforward."

## Shared elements across the site

These appear on every page so the visitor always knows where they are and how to get in touch.

- **Top navigation** — Logo on the left, page links in the middle (Home, About Us, Medical Biller, Virtual Staffing, RCM, Contact), and a "Get a Quote" button on the right. On mobile, the menu collapses into a clean drop-down.

## Voice & copy principles

- **Confident, not boastful.** We claim numbers we can defend (98%+ clean claim rate, 24–48h turnaround) — not vague superlatives.
- **Plain English.** No jargon dumps. Where industry terms are needed (RCM, ICD-10, CPT, AR), they're explained in context.
- **Outcome-led.** Every service page leads with the result for the practice (cleaner claims, faster cash, better-staffed front desk) before describing the mechanics.
- **Original copy.** All descriptions are written in Infividhya's voice — no text copied from competitor sites.
